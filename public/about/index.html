About Yapstats

Yapstats turns a chat export into recap cards: who talks most, when the chat wakes up, which emoji is worn out, and an award for every member. It is built by one independent developer in the UK.

How it works

You export a chat from WhatsApp or Instagram and open the file on the Yapstats home page. Your browser reads the file and draws the cards as images. There is no account, no app to install, and no server that receives your chat.

How the numbers are counted

  • Messages are counted per sender. Photos, voice notes and deleted messages each count as one message.
  • A conversation starts after 6 or more hours of quiet. Whoever speaks first gets the credit.
  • Reply time is the median time a person takes to answer someone else, and needs at least 10 replies to be shown.
  • Most-used words leave out common English words such as “the” and “and”.
  • Awards go to the person who really is top for that title. Everyone else gets something true about them alone.

If the export cannot prove a number, the card for it is left out.

What it costs

The recap is free. The Full recap is an optional one-off purchase that adds a personal card for each member and extra rankings. Tips are welcome and never required.

Privacy

Short version: your chat export is read by your browser on your own device. It is never sent to Yapstats or to anyone else.

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

Your chat export

  • The recap page reads the file you choose and draws your cards inside your browser.
  • The file and the cards are held in memory only. They are gone when you close or reload the page.
  • The recap page loads no outside code, no trackers and no ads. Its security settings block the page from contacting any other server.
  • Cards leave your device only when you choose to share or save them.

What is stored on your device

The recap page sets no cookies. If you buy the Full recap, your browser remembers one small entry so the purchase stays unlocked. It holds no personal information.

Hosting

The site is delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages and block abuse, and gives the site owner visit totals that do not identify you.

Payments

The Full recap is sold through Polar, which acts as the seller and handles payment and tax. Polar collects your email and payment details. Yapstats receives the order details needed to support your purchase, such as your email and order number, and never sees your card details. The payment page receives nothing about your chat.

Recap emails

If you choose email delivery at checkout, your browser uploads a ZIP of the generated cards to Yapstats’ private Cloudflare storage. The cards can contain participant names, rankings and derived statistics. The original chat export stays on your device. Yapstats uses the email address on the Polar checkout to send the link through Resend. The email contains a private link and no chat names or card attachments.

The download link expires after seven days and the archive is scheduled for removal. If you abandon checkout, the archive is removed after the checkout expires or fails. Yapstats removes the email address from temporary delivery storage after sending or after delivery attempts end. Keep the private link with the people you intend to share it with.

Tips

Tips go through Ko-fi, which handles the payment under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data is held about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Yapstats stores recap cards temporarily only if you choose email delivery. Contact hello@yapstats.tech. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Other people in your chat

A chat export contains other people’s names and messages. The cards can show names, counts and aggregate results such as frequently used words. Share them where the people in them would expect, which is usually the group itself.

How to export a WhatsApp chat

An export is a text file of one chat: every message with its sender and time. WhatsApp makes it for you, and it takes about a minute. This guide covers iPhone and Android, then the problems people hit most.

On iPhone

  1. Open WhatsApp and open the group chat you want.
  2. Tap the group name at the top of the screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Export Chat.
  4. Choose Without Media.
  5. In the share sheet, tap Save to Files, pick a folder you will remember, and tap Save.

The saved file is usually a .zip. Leave it zipped. Yapstats opens it as it is.

On Android

  1. Open WhatsApp and open the group chat you want.
  2. Tap the three dots in the top corner, then More.
  3. Tap Export chat.
  4. Choose Without media.
  5. Pick somewhere you can find the file again, such as your Files app, Drive, or an email to yourself.

On Android the file is usually a .txt. If you chose to include media it arrives as a .zip, which also works.

Why “without media”?

Photos, videos and voice notes make the file much bigger and slower to save. A recap only needs the messages, names and times. In the export, each photo or voice note is replaced by a short placeholder line, and Yapstats still counts it as a media message.

Common problems

I cannot find Export chat

Update WhatsApp, then look again in the same place: under the group name on iPhone, or under More on Android. Exporting works for one chat at a time, so open the chat first.

I saved the file and now cannot find it

On iPhone open the Files app and search for “WhatsApp Chat”. On Android open your Files or Downloads app and sort by newest.

The numbers look lower than I expected

A recap can only count what is in the file. Very long chats can come out shortened, and messages from before you joined the group are never included.

Some people show up as phone numbers

WhatsApp writes a phone number for anyone who is not in your contacts. In Yapstats, open Recap settings, then Edit names, and type the name you want on the cards.

The dates look odd in the file

The file uses your phone’s own date and time format, so 3/4 can mean March or April depending on your settings. Yapstats works out the order from the whole chat, and handles 12-hour and 24-hour clocks.

Is my chat private?

The export is a file on your own phone. When you open it in Yapstats, your browser reads it on your device. Once you have your cards you can delete the export file.

The cards name the people in your chat, so share them in the group they came from, and think twice before posting someone else’s numbers in public.

How to get your chat export

WhatsApp on iPhone

  1. Open the group chat and tap the group name.
  2. Tap Export Chat, then Without Media.
  3. Tap Save to Files, then choose the saved file here.

WhatsApp on Android

  1. Open the group chat and tap the three dots, then More.
  2. Tap Export chat, then Without media.
  3. Save the file to your phone, then choose it here.

Instagram DMs

  1. Open your profile menu and go to Accounts Centre or Meta Account settings.
  2. Under Your information and permissions, open Export your information and select Create export.
  3. Choose your Instagram profile and Export to device.
  4. Select Messages, set the format to JSON, choose a date range and start the export.
  5. When it’s ready, download the ZIP and open it here. To recap just one file, open its message_1.json instead.

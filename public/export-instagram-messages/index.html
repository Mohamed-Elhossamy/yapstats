How to export your Instagram DMs

Instagram does not export one chat at a time. You ask Meta for a download of your messages, wait for it to be prepared, then pick the conversation you want. The one setting that matters is the format: it must be JSON.

Request the download

  1. Open your Instagram profile, open the menu, and go to Accounts Centre (some accounts show Meta Account settings).
  2. Under Your information and permissions, open Export your information and select Create export.
  3. Choose your Instagram profile, then Export to device.
  4. Select Messages only. Leaving everything else out keeps the download small.
  5. Set the format to JSON, choose a date range, and start the export.

Wait, then download

Meta prepares the file in the background and tells you when it is ready. A small account can take minutes. A busy one can take much longer. When it is ready, download the .zip from the same screen and keep it zipped.

Open it in Yapstats

  1. On the Yapstats home page, tap Upload chat export and choose the .zip.
  2. Your download holds many conversations. Search the list and pick the group or chat you want.
  3. Tap Create recap.

If you have already unzipped the download, open the folder for one conversation and choose its message_1.json file instead.

Common problems

Yapstats says it cannot read the file

The most common cause is choosing HTML as the format. Request the export again and set the format to JSON.

The download is huge

That happens when everything is selected. Request a new export with only Messages ticked and a shorter date range.

A conversation is missing

Check the date range you picked. Conversations with no messages in that range are left out.

Names look different from what I see in the app

The export uses each person’s account name. Open Settings, then Edit names, to change what the cards show.

Is my chat private?

Your browser reads the download on your own device. The export stays on your device, and Yapstats has no accounts or logins. Delete the download when you are done, because it holds all the conversations you exported.
